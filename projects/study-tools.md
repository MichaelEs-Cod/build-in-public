# Study tools

Tools I built to study my Master's in AI (in Spanish) while I learn English.

| Tool | What it does | Real number |
|---|---|---|
| [Professor Claude](https://github.com/MichaelEs-Cod/professor-claude) | Class material becomes study pages in Spanish, Portuguese and English, with audio made on my PC | 11 pages and 1,986 sentences with audio for one class day |
| [Live lecture translator](https://github.com/MichaelEs-Cod/live-lecture-translator) | Spanish → Portuguese live, 100% on a 6 GB laptop GPU | ~2.1–2.3 s average delay |
| Study notebook | Every chat with Claude about a course is saved inside that course folder | Tested in 6 cases |
| Anki deck | 79 English conversation phrases with free neural voice | 158 cards, 99 audios |
| Master's tutor (Nyvon) | Reads a task on the campus, asks me questions, reviews my answer, submits only with my "yes" | Every answer names its source file |
| Kaggle exercise (Master's) | Joining sales with a customer table, with checks in the code | 6,031 wrong rows → 5,200 correct rows |

The AI wrote most of the code. I defined the format, the languages and the level, and I study with these tools every week.
