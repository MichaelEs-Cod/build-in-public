# Avatar and video factory

Content for my brand (@michaell.ia) made with code and AI, reviewed by me before anything goes live.

## Pieces

| Work | What it does | Real number |
|---|---|---|
| Avatar factory | AI image generation driven from Claude Code, green background cut by a Python script | 162 poses, 1024×1536 PNG |
| Reel line | Each word of the narration has its time; titles, poses and sounds land on the right word | Every reel at −14 LUFS, review score ≥ 8 before delivery |
| Reel from a real screen recording | A script finds each key press in the recording, so nothing on screen is invented | 11 key presses detected |
| AI "drone" video | Open video model on a 6 GB laptop GPU instead of a paid tool | From 225 s to 6 s per step |
| Kids channel tests | Frame-by-frame review of every AI clip before publishing | Errors caught before publishing, clips redone |
| AI Video Machine (with a business partner) | One topic becomes a short 3D video: script, animation, voice, captions, cover | 17 ready-made scenes |
| Video cuts (Nyvon) | Long videos become short cuts on my PC, with a safety filter | 3.7× faster after the filter work |

## What the AI did and what I decided

The AI wrote the code, generated images and clips and checked them frame by frame. I chose the style, approved every script and image, and decide what is published.
